float  duration,
TransitionType  ttype = TransitionType::OUT_QUAD,
const Tween::Callback &  callback = nullptr 
)

Rotates the node smoothly toward the target over duration seconds, interpolating the global rotation with the given transition curve. The animation is driven by a Tween registered in the engine context and keeps running until completion (or until killed/overridden by another tween).

Parameters
targetWorld-space point to look at.
durationAnimation duration in seconds (must be > 0).
ttypeInterpolation curve (default: OUT_QUAD).
callbackOptional function invoked when the rotation completes.
Returns
The registered Tween, for chaining or killing.
